# FAQ

🇩🇪 [Deutsche Version](Haeufige-Fragen)

**How do I move a tab freely and change its size?**
Tabs in the bar have fixed places. **Windows** can be moved and resized freely. Turn the tab into a window (*Edit → Show as: Own window*), open the chat (**T**) and drag the window by its title bar or its bottom-right corner. See [Chat windows and filters](Chat-windows-and-filters).

**A message ends up in the wrong tab. What can I do?**
The tab editor has a **live test**: paste the line from the chat and you instantly see whether it matches. Keep in mind: the *first* matching channel in the list wins, and window filters take priority over all tabs.

**I created a filter and want to get rid of it.**
Settings (**K**) → **Edit** on the window/tab → **Additional messages …** → delete the rule with **✕**. Or delete the whole window in the tab list with **✕** and **Save**.

**The window is gone / off screen.**
*Edit → Window … → Reset position and size*. Also check whether *While chat is closed* is set to *Only when chat is open*.

**Old messages appear twice.**
Another mod probably saves the chat as well. Turn off one of the two features, e.g. *Channels & history → Save messages*.

**Clicking a tab gives an error message from the server.**
The tab's *switch command* (e.g. `/ch global`) doesn't exist on this server. Change it in the tab editor, leave it empty or turn off *Channels & history → Switch channel on tab click*.

**I want the normal chat on one server.**
Settings → *Server profiles* → turn **ChatLanes** off for that server.

**Does ChatLanes work with the CubesideMod?**
Yes.

**Where do I report bugs or suggest features?**
In the [issues](https://github.com/0eiki0/chatlanes/issues) on GitHub.
